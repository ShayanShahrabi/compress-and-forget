The full `requirements.txt` also lists inference dependencies; it is not a universal historical runtime lockfile.

## Primary design and interpretation

The released data have 69,225 observations for 23,075 paired trial identities. Keys are `model, seed, attribute, interference_level, trial_idx`; `quant_mode` identifies a precision observation. `k` counts all chronological updates, including the correct final value; there are `k-1` stale values. The reconstruction uses `|`-delimited ordered histories.

Exact paired McNemar tests are primary. GEE uses robust standard errors clustered by shared trial identity. Older marginal/mixed-model reports are retained as historical analyses, not substitutes for revised paired inference. Headline condition selection was exploratory/post-pilot, not preregistered. Numeric effects and model/layer geometry directions are heterogeneous; these artifacts do not establish a causal mechanism or universal semantic specificity.

## Sampling: raw completions available, final audit producer not recovered

The included pilot has temperature 0.7, top-p 0.9, five completions per prompt and at most 12 new tokens. Qwen has 300 mood prompts at k=64; Mistral has 100 prompts at k=16; Phi has 500 prompts at k=8. Mistral's pilot is not its 1,200-prompt headline main condition.

`analysis/verified/followup_sampling_verified.{json,csv}` preserve the audited prompt-level estimates and uncertainty. Completions are averaged within prompt before paired prompt bootstrap: 10,000 resamples, NumPy `default_rng(20261023)` reset per contrast, percentile intervals. Separate five-seed t intervals are sensitivity summaries. Completion rows are not independent prompt replicates.

The exact standalone producer for these final audit exports is not recovered here; adding the raw completions does not itself recover that script. Existing runners or alternative analyzers are not claimed to reproduce these bootstrap intervals. Point estimates and pairing can be checked directly from the CSVs. Top-k and repetition penalty were inherited/unlogged; only Mistral has an archived runtime report. Differences from historical greedy runs cannot be attributed solely to decoding.

## Geometry: plot regeneration is not raw probe reproduction

`analysis/verified/followup_layerwise_verified.json` is a preserved, cleaned **legacy** geometry export. The figure helper reads `models[].cleaned_distinct_pairs_equal_prompt_seed.curves`. It uses within-condition value-pair cosine, not cross-precision cosine between matched vectors. Values are aligned to the last string occurrence (`prompt.rfind(value)`); self-string and mirrored pairs are removed. Aggregation is pairs within prompts, prompts within seeds, then equal seed weighting. Layer 0 is embedding; the final returned state may include output normalization. Curves are descriptive, without inferential bands.

Legacy coverage is 25 Qwen mood prompts and 100 each for Mistral and Phi across five seeds. The repeated-string sensitivity leaves seven Qwen prompts across four seeds. The raw geometry CSVs total approximately 543 MB (about 518 MiB); Qwen's two files individually exceed normal GitHub file limits. This update includes manifests and compact summaries, **not those raw CSVs or a public hosting location**. The exact final cleaned-audit producer is also unavailable.

`analysis/figure-data/fig_item3_probe_keylevel_delta.csv` is a separate centered-probe summary: occurrence-level pairs, held-out per-layer/per-precision/per-kind centering and five-seed t intervals. It is not the legacy curve estimand and is not generated by `build_figures.py`. Raw centered NPZ arrays and the exact modified execution-script hash are absent. Do not infer raw-state replication or causal mediation from the preserved table.

## Evaluator and runtime provenance

Available evaluator copies and released ordered vocabulary manifests differ. For example, the published evaluator has one `eager` candidate while released mood manifests preserve two, and Qwen's occupation manifest repeats `plumber`. Changing candidate contents/order or deduplicating pools changes seeded sequences. We retain the public evaluator and manifests rather than choosing a historical file by guesswork. Released trial histories and matching manifests are replay references, not proof of the exact original evaluator/runtime.

The main documented RTX 3090 runtime and later Blackwell sensitivity runtimes differ. Missing runtime snapshots and modified script hashes prevent exact historical regeneration. Historical descriptions of Item2/Item3 archives do not imply that their raw contents are present in this checkout. No new GPU check, historical-runtime replication, or recovery of missing artifacts is claimed.
